# Retired lessons

Tombstones for rules that consolidation merged, generalized, superseded, or retired. Rules here are
not consulted. Each entry records enough to restore the rule: revert the commit that retired it,
or copy the entry back in a commit that says why. Entries are appended by consolidation and never
removed.

Entry shape:

```markdown
### <the retired rule's heading, verbatim>
- Retired: <date> · Reason: <merged into | superseded by | retired as unused> <heading, when there is one>
- Evidence: <why, from the retros or the dedupe verdict>
- Lived in: <file> until commit <sha>
- Entry: <the full entry as it stood, indented>
```
