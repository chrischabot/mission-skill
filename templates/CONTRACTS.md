## How to change a frozen contract

1. A lane returns `BLOCKED(contract-change)` with: section, current text, proposed text, why, lanes affected.
2. The orchestrator decides (L/XL: adjudication at high effort; public API or datastore: human checkpoint per
   `orchestration.md` H1). Rejected → re-brief the lane with the reason.
3. Accepted → edit here, bump `Contract version`, add a Change log row with the D-entry, rerun contract tests, and
   re-brief every lane listed under "Read by" for the changed section. In-flight lanes of those sections are cancelled
   and restarted from their `progress.md`.
